    How to read this app

    Colours

    Action needed: the sensor is not delivering data, or has a serious fault or a critical battery.

    Attention soon: data is getting lost, the battery is low, measurements look unusual, or a breakdown is likely soon.

    Working fine.

    No information at this moment.

    Symbols

    On the map the ring around a symbol shows its condition: red, yellow or green.

    Battery

    The small battery under each sensor shows the battery condition and the measured value.

    12 V batteries (waste bins, water stations): shown as a band (good, fair, low or critical) with the voltage, because voltage cannot be turned into an exact percentage.

    Lithium batteries: their voltage stays flat until they are almost empty, so only “OK” is shown.

    “?”: the sensor does not report its battery.

    Directions

    Click a sensor, then 🧭 Directions. Google Maps opens in a new window with the route to the sensor. If the sensor has no GPS position in the data, the route goes to the area of the gateway that hears it best.

    📅 Choosing a moment

    Click 📅 to choose a day, then move the hour slider, or press ▶ to play that day hour by hour. The map shows the situation exactly as the system saw it at that moment. Now jumps back to the latest data.

    📶 Improve network

    Shows where sensors have a weak radio signal (measured, not guessed) and lets you drag a new gateway on the map to see which sensors would get a better signal or a backup receiver. ✨ Suggest best spot finds the place that helps the most sensors.

    🚐 Visit route

    In Check these first, switch to Visit route: the app puts all sensors that need a site visit into the shortest order and opens the route in Google Maps.

    Alerts

    The administrator gets an e-mail only when a sensor stops working or may stop working within 24 hours, once per problem. It contains the sensor, its location, a Google Maps route and what was detected. Several sensors with the same cause are combined into one e-mail. Everything else is shown here in the app.
